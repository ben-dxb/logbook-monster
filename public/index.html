    My meal plan

    Your plan is a set of day templates – one for each kind of day you eat differently on. Each has its own meals and targets. Add, rename, duplicate or delete templates below, or upload a plan (download it, change it in a spreadsheet and upload it again). Amounts are the starting point – each day still balances them to its targets. Days already in your history keep their own amounts.

    Read a PDF with AI

    Upload your coach's meal plan as a PDF (or a photo) and AI breaks it down into day templates, meals, foods, amounts and targets. New foods are added to My foods. Check everything before you save – nothing changes until you do.

    CSV format

    One row per food, with these headings in the first row: day_type, meal, meal_name, box, food, grams, locked, note. day_type is the name of a day template (a name you don’t have yet adds a new template); meal is the meal number; box is protein, carbs or fats (left blank, it follows the food's swap list); food is a food id or its name from My foods below; locked is yes to keep that amount fixed. Templates missing from the file keep their current meals. The easiest start is to download the current plan and edit it.

    Day templates

    My week and targets

    Which day template each weekday starts from. On the Daily Tracker you can switch any one day to another template.

    Day targets

    One row per day template. Protein is a minimum and fat a maximum; calories and carbs are matched.

    My free foods

    Whether sauces and green veg count towards the day’s macros. Ticked, foods marked Sauce or Green veg (in the foods below, in the food library and in the starter foods) add nothing to the totals and the balancing leaves them alone. Like the rest of your plan, it applies from the “Takes effect from” date, so earlier days keep the totals they had.

    My foods

    The foods your plan uses and lets you swap between. Values per 100g (or 100ml). Direct protein is the protein counted from meat, eggs, dairy and whey. Step is how amounts are rounded; min/max are per serving; daily order caps the day's total (for delivered meals). The swap list decides which box a food can be swapped in.

    Share with your coach

    Make a link your coach can open without a password or an account to see your days, weight, trends, supplements, gym sets and progress photos – read only, so nothing can be changed. It stops working when it runs out, or as soon as you switch it off here. Anyone holding the link sees the same, so only send it to your coach.

    Delete progress photos

    Delete all your progress photos taken between two dates, both days included. They’re gone for good – download any you want to keep first.

    AI

    Every AI feature in the app runs through OpenRouter on this one key. The key is kept on the server and never shown again – only you, the super admin, see these settings.

    Models

    Pick an OpenRouter model for each kind of task, e.g. a cheap text model for food values and a stronger one for reading PDFs. A task left blank uses the default model. Model names are as OpenRouter lists them, like anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.5.

    People

    Invite someone by email. They get a link to pick a password (or use Google with that address) and have their own days, history and weight. The link works once and runs out after 7 days. Invited people can sign in even if ALLOWED_EMAILS doesn’t list them.

    Email

    The email account Logbook Monster sends invites and notifications from. The password is kept on the server and never shown again.

    Using Gmail

    Gmail needs an app password, not your normal one: turn on 2-Step Verification for the Google account, then create an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste the 16 letters above. Server smtp.gmail.com, port 465. Outlook uses smtp.office365.com, port 587. Any other provider’s SMTP details work too.
