Privacy

What Logbook Monster keeps about you, where it goes and how to take it with you or delete it. This copy of the app is run by .

What is stored

  • Your account: email address, name (if you give one), a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), whether you sign in with Google, and your two-step sign-in key (sealed) if you turn it on.
  • What you log: your meal plan, days of food, weights, gym exercises, workouts and sets, food library entries, supplements and your daily checklist.
  • Progress photos: the pictures you upload, kept as files on the server’s disk, with their date, pose and note. Location and camera details are removed from each photo before it is kept.
  • Coach links you make, and when they were opened.
  • What an AI read you asked for found, until you add or drop it, and a count of AI calls a day for the app’s daily limits.
  • A security log of sign-ins (and failed tries), password changes, data downloads and similar events, with the network address they came from. It is kept for 180 days.

Foods and supplements you choose to share are shown to everyone with an account here; everything else is yours alone (and your coach’s, through a link you made).

What leaves the server

  • AI features: only when you ask (reading a plan or a supplement regime, estimating food values, telling a photo’s pose, a Timeline summary…), the text or file it needs – for a photo, only its small thumbnail – is sent to OpenRouter, which passes it to an AI model. With “Private AI only” on (the app’s setting), providers that keep or train on what they are sent are left out.
  • Email: invites and notifications are sent through the email provider the app is set up with.
  • Nothing else: no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no third-party requests from your browser.

Your data, your choice

  • Download everything as one file: account menu › Download my data.
  • Delete your account and everything in it, photo files included: Account admin › Delete my account. It can’t be undone.
  • Delete progress photos between two dates, or switch off coach links, on Account admin.
